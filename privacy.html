Privacy

Last updated 2026. B2 Ready UK is made by HB LABS.

The short version

B2 Ready UK has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Your progress, your writing and your recordings stay on your own device.

What stays on your device

The app saves your scores, drafts, study-plan ticks and settings in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Reset everything, or by clearing your browser data.

Microphone and speaking transcripts

The speaking recorder only uses your microphone after you press Record and your browser asks for permission. Recordings stay in your browser and are deleted when you leave the page. If your browser offers live transcription (Chrome and Edge do), your speech is converted to text by your browser's own speech service, which may process audio on its servers under the browser maker's privacy policy. We never receive your audio or transcripts.

Payments

Purchases are handled by our payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record and processes your name, email address and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the information needed to provide your order and support, such as your email address and order details. We never see your full card details.

Hosting

This website is served by a hosting provider that may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track you.

Contact

For any privacy question, reply to your purchase receipt and we will help.
