Questions and answers.

Is this the official test?

No. B2 Ready UK is an independent practice app from HB LABS. It isn't connected to the Home Office or to any test provider. It trains you in the task types and the B2 level used across the approved tests, with original questions written for B2 learners.

Which test does it prepare me for?

All the approved four-skill B2 tests: IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, LanguageCert Academic or General SELT, Trinity ISE II and Skills for English UKVI. Tell the app which one you're taking and it shows the score you need.

How do I get it after paying?

Straight away. Your receipt contains an unlock code and a download. Enter the code in the free app on any device to unlock everything there, or open the downloaded file on your laptop to use it offline.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the app in Safari or Chrome, enter your code, then add it to your home screen. It works like a normal app, even without signal. Speaking transcripts need Chrome or Edge and an internet connection.

Will it tell me my exact score?

It estimates your level in each skill from your results: below B2, borderline, B2 or above B2. It's a guide to help you decide when to book, not an official score.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Your progress, writing and recordings stay on your own device.

What if it's not for me?

7-day money-back guarantee. Just reply to your receipt email.
